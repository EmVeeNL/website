language: en
page: process
componentId: phases
heading: The phases
steps:
  - title: 1. Introductory conversation
    description: The first conversation is not a sales pitch. After this conversation, it should be clear whether a next step makes sense.
    list:
      - what is happening now
      - why change is needed
      - who uses the solution
      - what impact the problem has
      - what has already been tried
      - what expectations exist
      - whether EMVEE is the right fit
  - title: 2. Strategy
    description: Before design or development begins, we bring the core of the question into focus. Strategy does not make everything bigger. It helps us make fewer, better decisions.
    list:
      - the objective
      - the main users
      - the desired change
      - the constraints
      - the most important risks
      - what is needed now
      - what can consciously wait
  - title: 3. Advice
    description: EMVEE gives honest advice on the most sensible next step. Advice is only valuable when it leaves room for an outcome that may involve less work.
    list:
      - building a new product
      - improving an existing system
      - conducting further research
      - removing functionality
      - choosing a different technology
      - deciding not to build yet
  - title: 4. Concept
    description: The concept translates strategy into an understandable solution. The concept makes decisions visible before costly implementation begins.
    list:
      - information architecture
      - user flows
      - content structure
      - key functionality
      - technical direction
      - boundaries of the first version
  - title: 5. Design
    description: Design is about more than appearance. The design follows either the EMVEE Design System or the client's existing system.
    list:
      - hierarchy
      - navigation
      - interaction
      - states
      - accessibility
      - responsive behaviour
      - light and dark mode
      - the relationship between content and functionality
  - title: 6. Development
    description: During development, the solution is built step by step. Speed matters. But never at the expense of calm after delivery.
    list:
      - understandable architecture
      - maintainable code
      - limited dependencies
      - clear components
      - secure data handling
      - automated checks
      - documentation where needed
      - regular alignment
  - title: 7. Testing
    description: Testing does not begin at the end. Not every risk can be eliminated completely. But every important risk should be visible and consciously assessed.
    list:
      - functionality
      - forms
      - navigation
      - error handling
      - responsive behaviour
      - browsers
      - accessibility
      - performance
      - SEO fundamentals
      - privacy and security
      - content and links
  - title: 8. Launch
    description: Launch is a controlled transition. A deployment is only complete when the public solution has been checked and works as intended.
    list:
      - the production environment
      - domains and HTTPS
      - redirects
      - analytics where needed
      - monitoring
      - backup and rollback
      - content review
      - responsibilities
      - a smoke test after publication
  - title: 9. Aftercare
    description: Real-world experience often begins only after launch. Aftercare is not an afterthought. It is part of responsible delivery.
    list:
      - questions
      - small corrections
      - monitoring
      - unexpected behaviour
      - explanation
      - documentation
      - evaluation
  - title: 10. Long-term collaboration
    description: Not every client needs continuous development. It can still be valuable to have someone who understands the technical context and can help at the right moment. The collaboration remains intentional and proportionate to what is truly needed.
    list:
      - maintenance
      - technical guidance
      - periodic reviews
      - further development
      - performance monitoring
      - advice
      - support with new decisions
---
