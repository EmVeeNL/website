heading: What EMVEE believes in
items:
  - title: Calm
    description: Calm does not mean working slowly. It means decisions are clear, expectations are realistic and the project remains manageable.
  - title: Clarity
    description: Technology does not need to feel mysterious. EMVEE explains what is happening, why a decision is made and what its consequences are.
  - title: Trust
    description: Trust does not come from large claims. It grows through keeping commitments, naming risks honestly and taking responsibility.
  - title: Personality
    description: Digital products are used by people. That is why EMVEE looks not only at systems, but also at context, behaviour and daily practice.
  - title: Quality
    description: Quality is more than a polished interface. It also includes accessibility, performance, maintainability, privacy, security and transferability.
  - title: Simplicity
    description: Simplicity does not mean the challenge is easy. It means complexity is carefully understood and not unnecessarily passed on to the user.
