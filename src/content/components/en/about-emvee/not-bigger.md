heading: No bigger than necessary
body:
  - EMVEE does not aim to run as many projects as possible at the same time.
  - The preference is for suitable collaborations where attention, quality and responsibility remain possible.
  - "That also means saying no sometimes. For example, when:"
list:
  - the timeline leaves no room for quality
  - the question does not match the expertise
  - expectations are unrealistic
  - the collaboration is driven mainly by speed or price
  - another party is clearly a better fit
afterList:
  - An honest no protects both sides.
